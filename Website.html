Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Uphaar app free to use?


Yes! Listing items for donation and claiming free items is 100% free. We only charge a small nominal platform fee if you are renting premium clothing to ensure secure transactions.

How do you ensure user safety?


We use mobile number verification, require profile setups, and have an in-app rating system. We also provide a secure messaging system so you don't have to share your personal phone number.

Can NGOs register on Uphaar?


Absolutely. We have a dedicated dashboard for verified NGOs to post their urgent requirements (e.g., winter blankets) and receive bulk donations directly from users.

Who handles the delivery?


Currently, users coordinate self-pickups or drop-offs via the in-app chat. We are soon introducing a low-cost eco-friendly courier integration for city-wide deliveries.
