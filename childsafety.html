Uphaar Safety Standards

Child Safety Standards

Uphaar – Wall of Kindness is committed to creating a safe, respectful, and secure community for all users.

Zero Tolerance Policy

Uphaar strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, exploitation, harassment, or any harmful behavior involving minors.

Our Safety Commitments

  • We prohibit content involving child exploitation or abuse.
  • Users can report harmful content or abusive behavior directly within the app.
  • Accounts violating our policies may be suspended or permanently removed.
  • We cooperate with legal authorities when required by applicable laws.
  • We continuously improve moderation and community safety systems.

Reporting Concerns

Users are encouraged to report any inappropriate content, suspicious activity, harassment, or child safety concerns through the app's reporting system.

Community Guidelines

All users must follow community guidelines that promote respect, kindness, and safety. Any violation may result in content removal, account restriction, or permanent bans.
