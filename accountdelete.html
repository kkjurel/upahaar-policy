Delete Your Uphaar Account

Uphaar respects your privacy and gives you full control over your account and personal data. You can permanently delete your account directly from inside the app at any time.

How to Delete Your Account

1
Open the Uphaar app
2
Go to Settings
3
Tap Delete Account
4
Confirm your deletion request

What Data May Be Deleted

  • Profile information
  • Uploaded clothing listings
  • Messages and chat data
  • Saved preferences
  • Account authentication information

Data Retention

Certain information may be temporarily retained for legal compliance, fraud prevention, safety, moderation, or security purposes before being permanently deleted.

Need Help?

If you experience any issues deleting your account, you can contact our support team.
